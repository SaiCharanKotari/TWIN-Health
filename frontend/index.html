How Clinicians & Patients Interact

Both experiences are backed by the same local JSON records in data/ with zero client-only handoffs.

🩺 Clinician Workflow (/input)

  • 1 Target Patient Identification: Enter mandatory Patient Email Address at top of assessment workspace.
  • 2 Multi-Disease Inference: Enter vitals or upload CSV/Excel. Run 5 local ML pipelines in milliseconds.
  • 3 Persistent Results: View exact results at /results?assessmentId=<id> with full biomarker explanations.
  • 4 Activity Monitoring: Click Patient Activity Planning to view patient-reported habit completion and medication adherence in real time.
Open Clinician Workspace →

👤 Patient Experience (/patient)

  • 1 Email Sign-In: Patient logs in with the exact email entered during clinical assessment.
  • 2 AI Chronotherapy Routine: View personalized 24-hour schedule (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Bedtime).
  • 3 Interactive Checklists: Check off daily walking, exercise goals, hydration, and doctor-verified medications.
  • 4 Adherence Telemetry: Track 7-day adherence percentages persisted directly into backend JSON storage.
